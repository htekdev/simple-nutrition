How It Works

Every time you eat, build a box.

🥩

1. Anchor

Start with protein. It anchors your meal and keeps you full.

🥦

2. Fill

Add volume — vegetables, fruit, broth. More food, fewer calories.

🍚

3. Dial

Turn the portion dial on rice, pasta, bread. Protein stays. Portions move.

🌶️

4. Flavor

Big flavor, small budget. Salsa, spices, herbs, hot sauce — go wild.
